# Notes

Ideas and thoughts before they are research or a decision: what we might
build, why it might matter, and what it would take to find out. Nothing
here is authoritative, and nothing here is a commitment.

- One note per file, named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`.
- Append-only. A note records where a thought landed on its date; a later
  thought is a later note, not an edit.
- A note graduates to [`research/`](../research/README.md) when someone
  runs the experiment, and to an RFD when something costly to undo is
  about to depend on it.
- The book carries every note under Notes, through an include from
  `src/notes/`.
